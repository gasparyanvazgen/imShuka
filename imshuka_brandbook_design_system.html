imShuka / իմ շուկա

«Հայաստանի առցանց շուկա՝ փոքր խանութների համար»

Meaning “My Market” in Armenian, imShuka is a mobile-first marketplace connecting regional workshops, independent makers, and small local shops across Armenia’s marzes directly with everyday buyers—built around clarity, cultural warmth, and zero friction.

imshuka.am

Brand Pillars & Personality

How imShuka looks, speaks, and behaves
01

Warm & Crafted

Rooted in rich pomegranate crimson rather than cold corporate navy. Feels tactile, human, and respectful of Armenian craftsmanship.

02

Local & Authentic

Celebrates real makers from Dilijan to Gyumri. Every storefront carries its own clean link (imshuka.am/shop) and regional identity.

03

Trustworthy & Clutter-Free

No noisy flash-sale banners, dark patterns, or mandatory buyer sign-ups. Transparent pricing in AMD (֏) and verified store badges.

04

Mobile-First Simplicity

Designed thumb-first with 44px touch targets, bottom sheet drawers, and instant Instagram-to-storefront publishing in minutes.

Clearspace & Construction Grid

x = 24px clearspace

Maintain a minimum clearspace equal to 25% of the tile width around the mark. Corner radius is fixed at 22.9% (rx="22" on a 96×96 grid).

Do
  • Use pure white (#FFFFFF) strokes inside the #841838 rounded tile.
  • Preserve the rx="22" squircle proportion at all display sizes.
  • Pair with Plus Jakarta Sans Bold for the imShuka wordmark.
Don’t
  • Do not place gradients, drop shadows, or bevel effects on the arch mark.
  • Do not switch the primary brand tile to green (reserved strictly for verified badges).
  • Do not stretch, rotate, or alter the spacing between the arch and inner “i”.

06 / VOICE & MICROCOPY

Voice, Tone & Microcopy Guidelines

Armenian-first communication that feels neighborly, transparent, and action-oriented.

SELLER PROMISE
«0% տեղադրման վճար»

Zero listing fees — sellers keep 100% of their revenue.

BUYER FRICTIONLESS CTA
«Պատվիրել առանց գրանցման»

Order without registration — just name, +374 phone, and delivery choice.

PRIVACY ASSURANCE
«Գնորդներին չի ցուցադրվում»

Explicit reassurance that a seller's login phone stays private.

imShuka Voice (Clear & Respectful)
«Վերցնել այսօր Իջևանում»
Specific, local, and immediately useful to the shopper.
«Վաճառողը կզանգահարի պատվերը հաստատելու համար»
Sets clear human expectations after checkout.
Avoid (Aggressive / Jargon)
«ՇՏԱՊԵՔ! Մնացել է ընդամենը 1 հատ!!!»
Artificial urgency clashes with our calm, trusted market tone.
«Ստեղծեք աքաունթ և վերիֆիկացրեք Ձեր պրոֆիլը»
Avoid heavy transliterated tech jargon when natural Armenian works.
